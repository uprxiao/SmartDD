## 修改建议

1. 保持当前非 Darwin 生产门禁；不把 G340 的 5 项机制通过、1 项真实缺口改写为全绿。
2. 将前后 guard、Landlock/seccomp、匿名 staging 与版本保留继续作为各自覆盖范围内的措施，不宣称它们锁住宿主注册名称。
3. 只有找到可同时验证注册名称/根与必要祖先身份并完成交换的内核原语，或不扩张权限且约束外部改名者的强制设施后，才制定一项针对最终授权/提交边界的新隔离实验。当前不建议复跑旧 6 项。
4. 不以私有 mount 视图替代宿主 named-root 合同；不以系统 LSM、全局挂载、immutable/freeze 或约束全部写入者等新增部署条件绕开现有范围。

## 一致性校验

G340 源码中的独立 guard 与 renameat2、oracle 的 observed-gap 分支，以及已归档的 5 通过/1 缺口结论一致。fd-authority.c 明确限定读取与私有恢复权限，不能据此认定生产 source writer 已有防护实现。R07.17 的非 CAS/保留版本规则也未承诺阻止所有外部命名空间移动；恢复语义不能替代内核防止越权提交的证据。

## 严格缺项

- A supported unprivileged kernel/filesystem primitive that atomically validates issued root plus relevant ancestor/name identity and performs the source rename/exchange under that same authorization, or a mandatory namespace exclusion facility effective against the external actor without new privileges.
- A justified deployment/target-filesystem availability guarantee for that primitive on ordinary Linux desktop systems, with no container-only CAP dependency.
- A deterministic counterexample-resistant test of moves of root itself, relevant ancestors and the held parent, including same-parent rename/replacement, at the final authorization/commit boundary; no new such test executed.
- After any mechanism is actually proven: production source writer integration, durability/recovery and original fault obligations, actual FileService consumers and target packaged App/install validation remain separate and unexecuted by this review.
