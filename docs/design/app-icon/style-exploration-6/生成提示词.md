STYLE 01: BOLD SILHOUETTE. A single solid charcoal silhouette with rounded organic edges on plain white. A tiny ant viewed in a slight three-quarter side pose, small oval head and gently curved antennae, narrow little thorax and teardrop abdomen, slender lower legs firmly planted. Two forelimbs lift the bar overhead. No face, no internal lines, no fingers. Weights are two single thick rounded-rectangle solids at either end. The small rounded ant must look endearing even without a face. Visually very economical, iconic, strong size contrast, a more refined distinctive silhouette than a generic stickman. Totally flat: no tile, no depth, no gradients.
